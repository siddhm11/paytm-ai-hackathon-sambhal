    Sambhal ಸಂಭಾಲ್

    Paytm merchants repay their loans a little every day, straight from their sales. One bad week (a wedding, an illness, heavy rain) and the unpaid days pile up into one big deduction when the shop reopens.

    • Spots it early. Sales below half of normal two days running, before anything is missed.
    • Talks in the merchant's language. Kannada, Hindi or English, by voice, only between 8 am and 7 pm.
    • Offers only what the lender approved. The AI listens and talks. Code decides every rupee and every hour.
