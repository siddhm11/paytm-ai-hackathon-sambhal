Cue points to talk from, not lines to memorise. → advances a step.

**Before you start:** server running, browser at `localhost:8000`, Reset pressed, volume up, phone hotspot ready.
Lender console tab open. Check that the header chips say *Voice: Sarvam* (or *cached audio*).

**Close (one line):** Sambhal starts where the risk score stops: the conversation, the fix, and proof it worked.

## If a judge asks… (cue points)

* **"Isn't this a collections bot?"** It acts before the miss; collections start after. It can tell the lender to back off: show Lakshmi.
* **"Paytm already has risk scores."** A score says *who*. Sambhal finds out *why* (the wedding), offers the lender's fix, and proves recovery.
* **"What if the AI says something wrong?"** The LLM never decides amounts. If it rephrases, the check rejects new numbers or threats and the template goes out. Show V01–V05 on the Proof tab.
* **"What about fraud?"** Imran: sales collapse but "all fine" → review, no relief.
* **"Who pays?"** Lending partners: fewer loans slipping past due, lower cost per case than a call centre, RBI's rules (1 Jan 2027) built in.
* **"What's real?"** Voice, understanding, rules engine, ledger, tests are real. Merchants and the settlement feed are synthetic. Say it plainly.
* **"How would you pilot it?"** Merchants with Sambhal vs similar merchants without. Measure: overdue rate, biggest deduction, complaints, cost per case, violations (target 0).

## If something breaks

| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| Wifi dies | Flip **Offline** in the header. Rules + templates + cached audio carry the whole story. |
| Mic doesn't hear | Click the 🎙 chip with Ravi's line. |
| No sound | Read the English line under the bubble; keep going. |
| Page stuck | Reset, then click step dot 7 (it replays instantly). |
